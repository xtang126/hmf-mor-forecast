\section{Model}

\subsection{Halo mass function}

The comoving number density of haloes per unit halo mass is $dn/dM$,
computed from the Tinker et al.~(2008) fitting function via the
\texttt{hmf} package. The expected number of haloes with true mass in
bin $[M_j, M_{j+1}]$ and redshift in $[z_{\min}, z_{\max}]$ over a survey
of area $\Omega_s$ is
\begin{equation}
    N_j^{\rm true}(\Omega_m, \sigma_8)
    \;=\; V_{\rm shell}(\Omega_m, \Omega_s) \, \int_{M_j}^{M_{j+1}} \frac{dn}{dM}\, dM ,
    \label{eq:Ntrue}
\end{equation}
with the comoving volume of the shell
\begin{equation}
    V_{\rm shell}
    \;=\; \frac{\Omega_s}{\Omega_{\rm sky}}
    \bigl[ V_c(z_{\max}) - V_c(z_{\min}) \bigr],
    \qquad \Omega_{\rm sky} = 41253\,{\rm deg}^2 .
\end{equation}

\subsection{Mass--observable relation}

We adopt the standard log-normal MOR (Kaiser 1986; Stanek et al.~2006;
Evrard et al.~2014),
\begin{equation}
    \ln O \;=\; \ln A
    \;+\; \alpha \ln\!\left(\frac{M}{M_{\rm piv}}\right)
    \;+\; \beta  \ln\!\left(\frac{1+z}{1+z_{\rm piv}}\right)
    \;+\; \varepsilon,
    \qquad \varepsilon \sim \mathcal{N}(0,\, \sigma_{\ln O}^{2}) .
    \label{eq:MOR}
\end{equation}
Defining the \emph{inferred mass} $\hat M$ by inversion of the mean
relation and subtracting from Eq.~\eqref{eq:MOR}, the normalisation,
pivot, and redshift terms cancel, leaving
\begin{equation}
    \ln \hat M \;=\; \ln M \;+\; \frac{\varepsilon}{\alpha}
    \;\Longrightarrow\;
    P(\ln \hat M \,|\, \ln M)
    \;=\; \mathcal{N}\!\left(\ln M,\; \left(\sigma_{\ln O}/\alpha\right)^{2}\right).
    \label{eq:invert}
\end{equation}
Converting to base-10 logarithms,
\begin{equation}
    \boxed{\;\sigma_{\log_{10}\! M} \;=\; \frac{\sigma_{\ln O}}{\alpha \ln 10}
    \;\approx\; \frac{f_{\rm obs}}{\alpha \ln 10}\;},
    \label{eq:sigmalogM}
\end{equation}
where $f_{\rm obs} \approx \sigma_O/\langle O \rangle$ is the fractional
scatter in the observable (valid for $\sigma_{\ln O} \lesssim 0.3$).
Because Eq.~\eqref{eq:sigmalogM} contains only the ratio
$\sigma_{\ln O}/\alpha$, the forecast is observable-agnostic: $A$,
$M_{\rm piv}$, $\beta$, and $z_{\rm piv}$ do not enter.

\subsection{Migration matrix}

On a grid of $\log_{10} M$ bin edges $\{x_i\}$ with true-bin centres
$\bar x_j$, the probability that a true-bin-$j$ halo is placed in
observed bin $i$ is
\begin{equation}
    P_{ij} \;=\;
    \Phi\!\left(\frac{x_{i+1} - \bar x_j}{\sigma_{\log_{10}\! M}}\right)
    -\;
    \Phi\!\left(\frac{x_i     - \bar x_j}{\sigma_{\log_{10}\! M}}\right),
    \qquad \sum_i P_{ij} \equiv 1,
    \label{eq:migration}
\end{equation}
with $\Phi$ the standard-normal CDF. Two matrices are stored:
$\mathbf{P}_{\rm data}$ built from the true (data-generating)
scatter, and $\mathbf{P}_{\rm model}$ built from the scatter assumed
in the fit. The two need not be equal.

\subsection{Observed counts and likelihood}

The expected observed counts per bin are
\begin{equation}
    N_i^{\rm obs}(\Omega_m, \sigma_8)
    \;=\; \sum_j P_{ij}\, N_j^{\rm true}(\Omega_m, \sigma_8) .
    \label{eq:Nobs}
\end{equation}
Adopting a Poisson likelihood (independent bins, no shot-noise
covariance),
\begin{equation}
    \ln \mathcal{L}(\Omega_m, \sigma_8)
    \;=\; \sum_i \Bigl[
        N_i^{\rm data}\, \ln N_i^{\rm obs}
        \;-\; N_i^{\rm obs}
        \;-\; \ln \Gamma(N_i^{\rm data} + 1)
    \Bigr] .
    \label{eq:like}
\end{equation}
The mock data vector $N_i^{\rm data}$ is generated once at fiducial
$(\Omega_m^{\rm fid}, \sigma_8^{\rm fid})$ using
$\mathbf{P}_{\rm data}$; no Poisson noise is added, so the
recovered posterior is centred on the fiducial when the model is
correctly specified.

\section{Forecast scenarios}

Four assumption modes are supported by varying the model-side
scatter $f_{\rm obs}^{\rm model}$ relative to the data-side
$f_{\rm obs}^{\rm data}$:

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lll}
\toprule
Scenario                 & Setting                                                                 & Question addressed                             \\
\midrule
Correct assumption       & $f^{\rm model} = f^{\rm data}$, fixed                                   & Best-case statistical precision                \\
Ignore scatter           & $f^{\rm model} = 0$                                                     & Bias from neglecting scatter                   \\
Wrong scatter value      & $f^{\rm model} \ne f^{\rm data}$, fixed                                 & Sensitivity to miscalibration                  \\
Marginalise over scatter & $f^{\rm model}$ sampled with prior                                      & Realistic constraints with unknown scatter     \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\section{Outputs}

For each grid point the pipeline produces an MCMC chain in
$(\Omega_m, \sigma_8)$ (and $f_{\rm obs}^{\rm model}$ when
marginalising). The headline forecast products are:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item 68\% and 95\% credible contours in the $(\Omega_m, \sigma_8)$
          plane for each scenario.
    \item Bias of the posterior mean from the fiducial point, as a
          function of the mismatch $f^{\rm data} - f^{\rm model}$.
    \item Constraint area (or figure of merit) versus survey area
          $\Omega_s$ and mass threshold $M_{\min}$.
\end{enumerate}
